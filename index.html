About Me

I'm Umair Sial, a Pakistani independent astronomy enthusiast and BS Physics student at the Pakistan Institute of Engineering and Applied Sciences (PIEAS), Islamabad (2026–2030). My interests include astronomy, astrophysics, space science, and theoretical physics.

Alumnus of Daanish School Center of Excellence & Authority

I spent seven years as a boarding student at Daanish School Boys, where I developed discipline and the ambition to pursue astronomy. My physics mentor, Muhammad Farhan (MPhil Physics; specialist in Laser-Induced Breakdown Spectroscopy), helped shape my learning journey.

Education

I completed Matriculation in General Sciences with 91.25% (2022–2024) along with that I learned html and css independently. And Intermediate in Computer Science with 85% (2024–2026), through BISE Lahore. Alongside my studies in ICS, I collected Solar System planetary data, and wrote a collection of 60 articles. I prepared the architecture of my site that times and stated workking on it in December 2025 durirng my winter vacations.

Project | X HORIZON

During those winter vacations, I began turning my earlier website architecture into a project that would grow into X HORIZON. My vision is to make high-quality astronomy and space-science learning, simulations, resources, and a welcoming community accessible to enthusiasts without paywalls or a requirement for rewards. Although the project had an earlier identity, the core architecture and purpose were already taking shape.

Mentors of my Life

I have two mentors in my life that played a very important role in my life for astronomy and sciences.

Muhammad Farhan | MPhil PhysicsSpecialist in Laser-Induced Breakdown Spectroscopy (LIBS)

My teacher played a very important role by motivating and building my strong concepts in physics. A respectful, humble, and hardworking teacher who has always guided and supported his students. He explains every physics topic clearly and conceptually, creating a comfortable learning environment without unnecessary strictness. His dedication, patience, and supportive nature make him a truly inspiring mentor.

Clarissa Do Ó | B. Thomas Soifer Postdoctoral Fellow at Caltech Exoplanet Imaging, Orbital Dynamics & Future Space Telescopes

Dr. Clarissa Do Ó has been a major source of inspiration in my life and my journey toward astronomy. Her research at Caltech on directly imaging exoplanets and understanding planetary-system dynamics shows how curiosity and persistence can contribute to science. Beyond her research, the time and effort she invests in sharing scientific content and helping people learn have deeply influenced me. Her example teaches me that knowledge becomes even more meaningful when we use it to guide and inspire others, serve without expecting a reward, and never forget where we came from. As I study physics at PIEAS in Pakistan and build X HORIZON, I hope to follow that spirit by making astronomy more accessible and encouraging others who are beginning their own journeys.

My University Journey | PIEAS

Beginning my BS Physics journey at the Pakistan Institute of Engineering and Applied Sciences (PIEAS), Islamabad, is an important step toward my long-term goal of understanding the universe through physics, mathematics, and astronomy. My path has grown from learning independently and building X HORIZON to studying physics formally. At PIEAS, I want to strengthen my foundations, develop scientific and computational skills, and gradually work toward research in astrophysics and space science. I also want to carry forward the values that inspired me: curiosity, perseverance, humility, and sharing what I learn with others. My journey is still at its beginning, and I hope to use every opportunity to grow while helping other students see that their circumstances do not have to limit their ambitions.
