About Me

Hi! I'm Umair Sial, a Physics undergraduate at the Pakistan Institute of Engineering and Applied Sciences (PIEAS), with a strong interest in astronomy, astrophysics, space science, and scientific computing.

I'm building my foundations in physics, mathematics, and programming while exploring how scientific ideas connect to real astronomical observations and space missions. My long-term goal is to contribute to ambitious research and technology that expands our understanding of the Universe.

I'm especially interested in exoplanets, planetary systems, stellar physics, cosmology, space exploration, and computational methods that help us investigate the Universe.

Beyond studying physics, I founded X HORIZON to help make astronomy and space science more accessible through learning resources, practical projects, scientific exploration, and community.

Research Interests

My current focus is learning the scientific foundations and computational tools needed to take part in astronomy and astrophysics research. These are developing interests, not claims of completed professional research.

Physics & Mathematics

Scientific Computing

Learning Python, numerical methods, data analysis, and visualization to explore scientific questions and work with astronomical data.

Space Science & Exploration

Following space missions, observatories, instrumentation, and technologies that extend humanity's ability to observe and explore space.

Outreach

X HORIZON — Explore Nature Beyond the Horizon

X HORIZON is an independent initiative founded by me in December 2025 with a vision of making astronomy and space science welcoming and accessible to learners, enthusiasts, and curious minds.

The project aims to bring learning materials, guided concepts, skills, projects, scientific resources, and community into one connected ecosystem. Its mission is to encourage curiosity and help more people engage meaningfully with space science.
