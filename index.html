About Me

I'm Umair Sial, a Physics undergraduate at the Pakistan Institute of Engineering and Applied Sciences (PIEAS), interested in astronomy, astrophysics, space science, and scientific computing.

I'm building my foundations in physics, mathematics, and programming while exploring how scientific ideas connect to real astronomical observations and space missions. My long-term goal is to contribute to research and technology that expands our understanding of the Universe.

My interests include exoplanets, planetary systems, stellar physics, cosmology, space exploration, and computational methods.

I also founded X HORIZON to help make astronomy and space science more accessible through learning resources, projects, and community.
